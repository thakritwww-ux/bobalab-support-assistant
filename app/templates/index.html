ABOUT THIS SPACE

Useful information.
Honest boundaries.

BOBALAB is an AI customer-support assessment demo. It can explain reviewed visa information and help you think through a next step. It can make mistakes.

Sources, with context

The reviewed collection includes a general DTV overview, selected Kuala Lumpur guidance and basic residence-reporting information. It is not a complete checklist for every country. A source link does not verify every claim in a reply.

A conversation, not a case tracker

This demo cannot inspect documents, confirm payments, book travel, submit applications or contact a team. No referral or follow-up is sent.

Keep it hypothetical

Use made-up examples. Do not send passports, bank statements, transfer receipts or other private documents. Messages go to Google’s Gemini API. This app keeps the chat in this tab’s memory; the provider’s data handling is separate.

Free service, sometimes a wait

The host can take time to wake up and the model has usage limits. A loading indicator does not mean a consultant is reviewing your enquiry.
